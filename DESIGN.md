# The writing room

A personal workbook, with the writing taking up more space than the interface.

- Paper white #ffffff, desk grey #f5f6f4, ink #27322e, secondary ink #56635b, forest #355b49, pale sage #e9efe9.
- Georgia for the reading and writing surfaces; the system sans-serif for orientation and controls. No network font dependency.
- A narrow, left-aligned reading column inside a broad desk. A simple ruled margin and generous blank space evoke an open notebook. The home page opens with a sentence being revised, showing the actual work of the course.
- One primary home action; curriculum appears as a sequence of skills, not achievement cards. No animations or numeric quality scores.

Review against the brief: neutral colours, generous spacing, editable plain text, no decoration competing with the writing. The single visual signature is the marginal revision example. Lesson numbers only express the real sequence. A warm-paper/terracotta palette was avoided in favour of white and green-grey so the room stays quiet and utilitarian.
